You have written the profile; this reads it. Turtle or JSON-LD — whichever your file is, it is recognised by looking at it rather than by asking you. What it holds is drafted into the submission form for you to check, and nothing is saved until you press Submit there.

Haven't got one? Fill in the submission form and press Download profile — you get the file to host beside your plugin, and nothing is saved here. What a profile is for, and where to put it.

Parameters and ports are not read from here. They live in your plugin's own bundle, in the vocabulary this catalogue already uses, so they are read from the bundle rather than retyped — see plugin profiles.
