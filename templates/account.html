What a plan is for

The catalogue is free and open to everyone, and always will be — all {{corpus}} plugins, the search, the API, the dumps and the SPARQL endpoint, with no account and no key. A plan pays for the server and buys promoted placement for plugins you make.

Promotion is bounded and labelled, and the ranking effect is published. It never changes a fact about a plugin, never hides anybody else's, and never touches a measurement.
